# Sales Invoice Report Update

Added a professional A4 sales invoice print report to the Sales module, based on the provided Arabic invoice form.

## Included
- Company information and tax number
- Invoice number, date, reference/order number and payment method
- Customer information
- Shipping information
- 10-row minimum item grid with code/name/specification/unit/quantity/unit price/total
- Discount, taxable subtotal, tax and grand total
- Notes section
- Five signature areas
- A4 RTL print layout
- Print preview from the Sales > Sales Invoices list

The feature is additive and does not alter existing invoice posting, inventory, accounting, treasury or customer balances.
